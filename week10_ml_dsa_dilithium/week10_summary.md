# Week 10 Summary

This week I studied ML-DSA and its connection to the earlier lattice topics.

## ML-DSA and Dilithium

ML-DSA is the NIST standardized lattice-based digital signature scheme
developed from CRYSTALS-Dilithium.

Unlike ML-KEM, which provides key encapsulation, ML-DSA is designed for
digital signatures.

## Main ideas studied

- key generation
- signing
- verification
- polynomial arithmetic
- challenge values
- bounded noise
- rejection and hint concepts

## What I learned

ML-DSA uses structured lattice mathematics together with polynomial
operations and hashing to build a post-quantum signature scheme.

The scripts in this week are simplified examples for understanding the
concepts. They do not implement the ML-DSA standard.
